Support

Effective date: October 3, 2026

NurseStamp saves personal time markers on Apple Watch and shows your timeline and shifts on iPhone. It requires iOS 18 or later and watchOS 11 or later. A paired iPhone is needed to install and set up the Watch app. No account or login is required.

Set up your Watch

  1. Install NurseStamp on your iPhone.
  2. Open the Watch app on iPhone and install NurseStamp on the paired Watch if needed.
  3. Open NurseStamp on both devices.
  4. Tap MED, MD, POSITION, PAIN, or CALL on Watch. Check that the app confirms the marker was saved.
  5. Open Timeline on iPhone to review transferred markers.

Markers saved on Watch transfer to iPhone automatically in the background when the paired devices can connect. You can save markers while your phone is away.

On your watch face

Add the NurseStamp complication to a supported watch face. It can show your most recent marker category and the time since it. Tap it to open the capture screen.

Anyone who can see your watch face may see this information. The complication's information stays on the device.

Review or change a marker

Open Recent on Watch or tap a marker in Timeline on iPhone. You can add an optional preset detail or delete a marker. Changing a detail keeps the original capture time. PAIN values are user-entered labels, not readings made by the app.

A marker isn't on iPhone yet

  1. Check Recent on Watch to confirm the marker was saved.
  2. Bring the paired devices together and open NurseStamp on both. Transfers may be delayed while either device is unavailable.
  3. In NurseStamp's Settings on iPhone, tap Try sending again or Check for updates.

Keep both apps installed while troubleshooting. If a capture wasn't saved, use its save-again button under Not saved in Recent. Don't assume an unsaved marker is in the timeline.

Review a shift or share a summary

Open Shifts on iPhone to review your shifts. Use End Shift to end an active shift.

Use the share icon labeled Export shift summary in Timeline, or Export this shift in a shift. In Export, choose a shift and PDF, plain text, or CSV format. Review the preview before sharing.

Exports are snapshots. Markers still on an offline Watch won't be included until they transfer. Shared files don't update later. Choose an approved destination and check times before using them in the medical record.

Privacy and deletion

There are no accounts, ads, analytics, patient fields, or free-text notes. Markers stay on the paired devices unless you choose to share a file. Read our Privacy Policy.

You can delete markers and shifts in the app. In NurseStamp's Settings on iPhone, Delete all markers removes all shifts and markers currently on iPhone. Deletions transfer to Watch when it reconnects.

Markers that haven't yet transferred from an offline Watch may appear later and need separate deletion. Shared files must be deleted at their destinations.
