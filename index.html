NurseStamp

Time markers for nurse shifts

  • One tap on Apple Watch

    Save a category and time, even when your iPhone isn't nearby.

  • Timeline and shift summary on iPhone

    Review your markers and share a summary when you choose.

  • Private by design

    No accounts, ads, or analytics. Your markers stay on your paired devices unless you choose to share a file.

Requires iOS 18 or later and watchOS 11 or later.
