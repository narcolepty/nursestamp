Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

NurseStamp is made by Howard Tseng. It helps nurses save personal time markers on Apple Watch and review them on iPhone.

Your markers stay with you

NurseStamp doesn't collect or send your app data to us. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, or cloud service. It doesn't access HealthKit, contacts, location, photos, or your microphone.

The app saves the category and time of each marker, optional preset details, shifts, and local sync information on your iPhone and paired Apple Watch. Preset details may include a position, an MD event, or a pain value that you select. These aren't measurements made by the app. Settings are stored locally too.

NurseStamp has no fields for patient names, identifiers, or free-text notes. Don't use it to store patient information. Follow your workplace's policies for personal devices and sharing files.

Transfer between your devices

Markers saved on Watch transfer to iPhone automatically in the background when the paired devices can connect. NurseStamp uses Apple's paired-device connection. It doesn't send markers to a developer server.

You can save markers without your phone nearby. An offline Watch may have markers that haven't reached iPhone yet.

On your watch face

The NurseStamp watch face complication can show your most recent marker category and the time since it. Anyone who can see your watch face may see this information. The complication's information stays on the device.

Storage and deletion

Marker files use Apple's file protection and are excluded from system backup. Temporary export files are also excluded from backup. NurseStamp doesn't offer cloud backup or account recovery. Removing the app or losing a device can mean losing markers that haven't transferred or been exported.

You can delete individual markers or shifts on iPhone. In Settings, Delete all markers removes all shifts and markers currently on iPhone. Deletions transfer to your paired Watch when the devices can communicate.

Markers that an offline Watch hasn't sent yet may appear later and may need to be deleted too. Deleting app data doesn't delete files you've already shared.

Files you choose to share

You can create a PDF, plain-text, or CSV snapshot and choose where to send or save it using Apple's share sheet. A file may include timestamps, categories, preset details, shifts, and sync identifiers. Review it and choose a destination approved by your workplace.

Shared files can leave your devices. The receiving app or service's privacy policy applies to its copy. We don't receive your export unless you separately choose to send it to us. A snapshot doesn't update when more Watch markers arrive. A shared file can't be taken back through NurseStamp.

The app removes its temporary export file after sharing and cleans up leftover temporary exports on later launches. If cleanup fails, Settings offers Remove temporary exports.

Apple services and support

Apple may process App Store purchases or downloads and device diagnostics under Apple's own settings and privacy policies. NurseStamp doesn't include an analytics or crash-reporting service that uploads your markers to us.

If you contact us for support, we receive the message and contact information you choose to provide. We use them to respond and resolve your request. Don't send patient information, real marker exports, or screenshots containing sensitive information. Use synthetic examples.

Ask through the same contact channel if you'd like your correspondence deleted. Legal obligations may require some records to be kept.

This website

This site uses no analytics, cookies, external fonts, scripts, CDNs, or trackers. We don't use it to collect app markers. The hosting provider may process website requests under its own privacy policy.

Children

NurseStamp is designed for nursing work. It doesn't collect personal data through the app, including from children.

Changes and contact

We'll update this page and its effective date if the app's privacy practices change.
